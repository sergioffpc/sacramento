## Verification methods

**Inspection** — Examine a document, configuration, content artifact, platform, deployment, or persistent state against explicit required properties without relying on subjective judgment.

**Automated Test** — Exercise the system under controlled initial conditions and compare observable results with deterministic or bounded pass/fail criteria.

**Analysis** — Calculate, reconstruct, or compare behavior and recorded results against approved profiles, models, tolerances, and reference data.

**Demonstration** — Execute and observe an end-to-end user or operational flow when successful integration and externally visible behavior are the acceptance concern.

**Representative Evaluation** — Obtain structured judgment from qualified Representative Evaluators when military training validity cannot be established through objective technical evidence alone.

An assignment may use multiple methods. It MUST identify one or more Required methods, zero or more Supporting methods, the required evidence, and the evidence owner.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-METHOD-001** — Every obligation MUST receive at least one Required verification method.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-METHOD-002** — Every Required method assigned to an obligation MUST produce obligation-level `Pass` before that obligation can receive `Pass`.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-METHOD-003** — A Supporting method MAY add corroborating evidence but MUST NOT produce obligation-level `Pass`, compensate for a failed or blocked Required method, or be required for acceptance.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-METHOD-004** — When more than one default assignment rule applies to an obligation, their Required methods MUST accumulate unless an approved Verification Method Exception explicitly removes one.

### Default assignment rules

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-INSPECTION-001** — Inspection MUST be a Required method for obligations concerning documents, declared content or configuration, target platforms, deployment properties, and other directly observable structural constraints.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-TEST-001** — Automated Test MUST be a Required method for deterministic state transitions, functional rules, boundaries, and prohibited behavior that can be exercised through controlled inputs.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-ANALYSIS-001** — Requirements governing physics, ballistics, Acoustic Propagation, Blast Overpressure, explosions, or injury outcomes MUST use both Analysis against versioned Approved Profiles and Automated Test under reproducible conditions as Required methods.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-DEMONSTRATION-001** — An assignment for a complete operational flow or access-mode parity MUST include Demonstration as a Supporting method for process completeness; its result is non-dispositive under `PROCESS-VERIFICATION-METHOD-003` and MUST NOT replace objective evidence.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-EVALUATION-001** — Representative Evaluation MUST be assigned as a Required method only when military validity, tactical perception, or the adequacy of represented behavior cannot be established from objective technical evidence alone, and all applicable technical methods MUST also remain Required.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-APPLICABILITY-001** — Before a verification procedure is approved, the method assignment for every stable obligation key MUST record whether its acceptance depends on tactical adequacy, human perception, credible military behavior, or validity for military training that objective technical evidence alone cannot establish, together with the rationale for that conclusion.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-APPLICABILITY-002** — When any dependency stated by `PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-APPLICABILITY-001` applies, Representative Evaluation MUST be a Required method for that obligation.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-APPLICABILITY-003** — Representative Evaluation MUST NOT be required solely because an obligation concerns a military subject when its complete acceptance criteria and tolerances are determined by Approved Profiles and reproducible objective technical evidence.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-APPLICABILITY-004** — If the method-assignment review cannot establish that objective technical evidence is sufficient, Representative Evaluation MUST be assigned as a Required method.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-INVENTORY-001** — The versioned Assignment Expansion Inventory MUST classify every normative identifier as `Required Within Identifier` when one or more of its obligations require Representative Evaluation, or as `Not Required for Identifier` only when objective technical evidence is sufficient for every obligation within it.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-INVENTORY-002** — `Required Within Identifier` MUST identify the matching assignment rows whose Required methods include Representative Evaluation and MUST use those rows' required-evidence statements as the identifier-level rationale; it MUST NOT make Representative Evaluation applicable to an independently objective obligation within a compound identifier.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-INVENTORY-003** — `Not Required for Identifier` MUST state that assigned objective criteria and reproducible technical evidence are sufficient for every obligation under `PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-APPLICABILITY-003`; an ambiguity finding or other non-obligation metadata entry MUST instead use `Not Applicable — Metadata`.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVALUATION-INVENTORY-004** — Before each verification procedure is approved, the implementation team MUST enumerate its independently fail-able stable obligation keys and replace the identifier-level classification with an explicit `Required` or `Not Required` decision and rationale for each key; mixed decisions within a compound identifier are permitted and MUST remain separately attributable in the procedure and evidence.

**Verification Method Exception** — A pre-procedure assignment decision that determines a default Required method is not applicable to one identified obligation. It is distinct from a Procedural Deviation and cannot alter an explicitly assigned method or an approved procedure execution.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-DEVIATION-001** — An assignment that departs from a default MUST use a Verification Method Exception that identifies the obligation key, default method, objective non-applicability rationale and evidence, remaining Required methods, decision date, and project-owner approval before the verification procedure is approved.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-DEFAULT-DEVIATION-002** — A Verification Method Exception MUST NOT remove a method explicitly required by a requirement or assignment, leave an obligation without a Required method, or be approved while method applicability remains uncertain.

## Evidence records

**Evidence Dependency Inventory** — The approved, versioned inventory of every registered changeable artifact, verification dependency, and directed dependency relation used to determine which accepted evidence a change may affect.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CONTENT-001** — Each verification execution MUST produce a versioned evidence record containing the verified requirement identifiers, exact product version, applicable Scenario, Map, content and profile versions, relevant platform, hardware and configuration, procedure and input data, execution date, executor, raw results, disposition, and approved procedural deviations.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DISPOSITION-001** — Each evidence record MUST assign exactly one calculated disposition to every identifier it verifies: `Pass`, `Fail`, or `Blocked`.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DISPOSITION-002** — A `Blocked` disposition MUST identify the unavailable dependency, unresolved ambiguity, invalid environment, or other condition that prevented a valid pass-or-fail decision.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-OBLIGATION-001** — Before execution, each verification procedure MUST enumerate every independently fail-able obligation within each covered identifier and assign each obligation a stable key that remains unchanged while that obligation's meaning remains unchanged.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-OBLIGATION-002** — The evidence record MUST assign `Pass`, `Fail`, or `Blocked` separately to every enumerated obligation and MUST retain observations and results attributable to that obligation key.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-OBLIGATION-003** — An identifier disposition MUST be calculated as `Pass` only when every enumerated obligation is `Pass`; as `Fail` when at least one obligation is `Fail`; and as `Blocked` when no obligation is `Fail` and at least one obligation is `Blocked`.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-OBLIGATION-004** — An identifier MUST NOT receive `Pass` while any normative clause lacks an enumerated obligation key or obligation-level `Pass` evidence.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-RETENTION-001** — Every accepted and superseded evidence record MUST remain immutable, retained indefinitely, and traceable to the requirement, product, content, profiles, procedure, and decision for which it was produced; this baseline authorizes no deletion or silent overwrite.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-GROUPING-001** — One verification procedure MAY cover multiple related requirement identifiers when the procedure states every covered identifier before execution.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-GROUPING-002** — A grouped procedure MUST retain separately attributable observations, results, and `Pass`, `Fail`, or `Blocked` dispositions for every covered identifier; an aggregate result MUST NOT substitute for identifier-level evidence.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-001** — The Evidence Dependency Inventory MUST enumerate every registered current requirement identifier, obligation key, product component and configuration item, Scenario, Map, content item, Approved Profile, verification procedure, input-data set, verification environment, and evidence record, together with every known directed dependency relation among them.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-002** — Every artifact or input used to produce, interpret, or accept verification evidence MUST be registered in its applicable authoritative inventory and classified in the Evidence Dependency Inventory before that evidence can receive `Pass`.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-003** — Before use in impact analysis, the implementation team MUST reconcile the Evidence Dependency Inventory against the complete current authoritative inventory for every category in `PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-001`, verify every procedure's declared inputs, outputs, and dependencies, and obtain project-owner approval for the exact inventory version.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-004** — Adding, removing, reclassifying, or changing an inventory item or dependency relation MUST create a new Evidence Dependency Inventory version and trigger re-evaluation of every retained impact analysis that relied on the preceding inventory version.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-005** — A missing, stale, or unclassified item or dependency relation MUST make affected-set membership uncertain and MUST NOT support an `Unaffected` classification.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-006** — Before executing a verification, inventory validation, or impact analysis, the planned output-record identifiers, classifications, and dependency relations MUST be registered in the candidate Evidence Dependency Inventory version used by that execution.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-007** — Populating an immutable pre-registered output record with its execution results MUST NOT create a new Evidence Dependency Inventory version when its identifier, classification, and dependency relations remain unchanged.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-008** — Discovery or production of an unregistered output, classification, input, or dependency relation MUST create a new candidate Evidence Dependency Inventory version and MUST classify affected-set membership as uncertain until the new version is coverage-validated and all required verification is rerun.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-DEPENDENCY-009** — Inventory validation and dependent impact-analysis re-evaluation MUST terminate only at an approved Evidence Dependency Inventory version that already registers every output record, classification, input, and dependency relation produced or discovered by that validation and re-evaluation and has no pending unregistered output.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-001** — An accepted verification MUST be executed again when a change to the product, Scenario, Map, content, approved profile, verification environment, input data, or procedure can affect its result.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-002** — Existing evidence MAY remain valid after a change only when a recorded impact analysis identifies the change and uses the current approved Evidence Dependency Inventory to trace it to every reachable requirement identifier, obligation key, verification procedure, verification environment, Approved Profile, content item, and evidence record.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-003** — An impact analysis MAY classify an accepted result as `Unaffected` only when no dependency path from the change to that result exists in the current approved and coverage-validated Evidence Dependency Inventory, or when reproducible analysis demonstrates that the accepted result is invariant under the change despite such a path.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-004** — An accepted result is invariant only when the changed baseline continues to satisfy the exact obligation-level acceptance criterion and retains a `Pass` disposition; raw observations MUST remain identical only when the applicable obligation or acceptance criterion requires exact identity.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-005** — An `Unaffected` classification MUST identify its inputs, reasoning or executable analysis, results, applicable changed baseline, obligation-level acceptance criterion, disposition, and Evidence Dependency Inventory version and MUST receive project-owner approval.

**PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-006** — Missing, stale, or unclassified dependency information, uncertainty about affected-set membership, or inability to establish that an accepted result is `Unaffected` MUST classify the verification as affected and require its execution again against the changed baseline.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-001** — A procedural deviation MUST NOT weaken, remove, reinterpret, or replace a normative obligation, acceptance criterion, Required verification method, or required evidence item.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-002** — A planned procedural deviation MUST identify the affected procedure version, requirement identifiers and obligation keys, rationale, changed steps, and evidence impact and MUST receive project-owner approval before execution; before approval, prospective preserved coverage MUST be established under `PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-001` and `PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-003` using the current approved Evidence Dependency Inventory.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-003** — An unplanned deviation observed during execution MUST assign `Blocked` to every affected obligation result until its impact is resolved.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-004** — Evidence from an unplanned deviation MAY be retained for acceptance only when recorded impact analysis under `PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-001`, `PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-002`, and `PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-002` through `PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-006`, using the current approved Evidence Dependency Inventory, demonstrates equivalent coverage and the project owner approves that analysis.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-005** — When equivalent coverage cannot be demonstrated and approved, the affected verification MUST be executed again under the approved procedure or a separately approved planned deviation.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-006** — After executing an approved planned deviation, the actual steps, controlled conditions, boundary cases, observations, and evidence outputs MUST reconcile to its approved prospective mapping before any affected obligation can receive `Pass`; any unapproved difference from that mapping MUST be treated as an unplanned deviation under `PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-003` through `PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-005`.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-001** — Equivalent coverage MUST preserve the exact affected obligations, acceptance criteria, Required methods, required evidence items, controlled-condition classes, boundary cases, and attributable observations and dispositions of the approved procedure.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-002** — For an unplanned deviation or another post-execution evidence-retention decision, the coverage analysis MUST provide a reproducible mapping from every affected approved-procedure element to its deviated execution evidence; any missing mapping, uncertainty, changed acceptance meaning, or inability to retain the same obligation-level `Pass` boundary MUST prohibit evidence retention and require rerun.

**PROCESS-PROCEDURE-DEVIATION-COVERAGE-003** — For a planned deviation, the prospective coverage analysis MUST provide a reproducible mapping from every affected approved-procedure element to the corresponding proposed step, controlled condition, boundary case, observation, and planned evidence output without requiring execution before approval.


## Responsibilities and approval

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-EVIDENCE-OWNER-001** — The implementation team, supported by AI agents, MUST produce and retain the technical evidence required by each assigned verification procedure.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-MILITARY-EVIDENCE-001** — Representative Evaluators MUST produce the structured military-validity evidence for every requirement whose acceptance assignment includes Representative Evaluation.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-APPROVER-001** — The project owner MUST make the final decision to accept or reject each requirement and its submitted evidence.

**PROCESS-VERIFICATION-SEPARATION-001** — Project-owner approval MUST NOT substitute for required Representative Evaluation evidence.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-TOLERANCE-001** — Before a Mode Equivalence procedure is executed, the implementation team MUST record proposed tolerances for each compared information item and tactically relevant outcome.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-TOLERANCE-002** — Representative Evaluators MUST confirm that differences within the proposed tolerances are tactically immaterial, and the project owner MUST approve those tolerances before comparative results are observed.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-TOLERANCE-003** — A tolerance changed after comparative results have been observed MUST invalidate those results and require a new verification execution under the approved replacement tolerance.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-COVERAGE-001** — Each Mode Equivalence procedure MUST use a versioned coverage inventory that enumerates every applicable Scenario-relevant information item, tactically relevant outcome, and canonical initial-state class for every covered requirement and Scenario capability.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-COVERAGE-002** — Every coverage-inventory item MUST trace to the stable requirement identifiers and Scenario capabilities that justify its inclusion, and every covered requirement and capability MUST trace to at least one applicable information item, outcome, or explicit non-applicability record.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-COVERAGE-003** — Before tolerances are finalized or comparative results are observed, the implementation team MUST reconcile the coverage inventory against the current requirements and Scenario-capability inventory, at least two qualified Representative Evaluators MUST confirm its tactical completeness independently, and the project owner MUST approve the exact coverage-inventory version.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-COVERAGE-004** — A Mode Equivalence obligation MUST NOT receive `Pass` unless every applicable item and canonical initial-state class in the approved coverage-inventory version has received the results required by its assigned methods.

**PROCESS-MODE-EQUIVALENCE-COVERAGE-005** — A change to a covered requirement, Scenario capability, information item, outcome, canonical initial-state class, or dependency MUST trigger impact analysis of the coverage inventory and retained Mode Equivalence evidence under `PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-001` through `PROCESS-EVIDENCE-CHANGE-006`.

**PROCESS-EVALUATOR-QUALIFICATION-001** — Before a Representative Evaluator observes the product or evaluation results, the evaluator MUST have a recorded qualification identifying the person, relevant armed-forces experience, applicable roles, environments, equipment and decisions, and the dates of that experience.

**PROCESS-EVALUATOR-QUALIFICATION-002** — Each Representative Evaluation procedure MUST define the required evaluator-experience scope and recency before candidate evaluators are selected or observe the product or results.

**PROCESS-EVALUATOR-APPROVAL-001** — The project owner MUST approve each Representative Evaluator against the recorded procedure-specific scope and recency conditions before that person's findings become acceptance evidence.

**PROCESS-EVALUATOR-SCOPE-001** — A Representative Evaluator's findings MUST be used as acceptance evidence only for evaluation questions within that evaluator's approved experience scope.

**PROCESS-EVALUATOR-EVIDENCE-001** — Each Representative Evaluation evidence record MUST remain traceable to the applicable evaluator qualification, procedure version, scope, recency conditions, and project-owner approval.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-PROTOCOL-001** — Before Representative Evaluators observe the product, each Representative Evaluation procedure MUST define its tasks, questions, controlled conditions, scoring method, and identifier-level acceptance criteria.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-PROTOCOL-002** — The project owner MUST approve the exact Representative Evaluation procedure version before any result produced by that procedure can become acceptance evidence.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-INDIVIDUAL-001** — The evidence record MUST preserve each evaluator's individual observations, scores, and findings separately from every aggregate result.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-AGGREGATION-001** — When an aggregate result is used, its calculation and pass/fail interpretation MUST be defined in the approved procedure before observations begin and MUST remain reproducible from the retained individual results.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-CONSENSUS-001** — Post-observation discussion or consensus MAY be retained as supporting evidence but MUST NOT replace, alter, or erase the original individual results.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-PANEL-001** — Every obligation for which Representative Evaluation is a Required method MUST be evaluated by at least two Representative Evaluators qualified for that obligation's evaluation scope.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-PANEL-002** — Each Representative Evaluator MUST complete and submit the individual result before observing another evaluator's result or participating in discussion about the evaluated behavior.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-PANEL-003** — Unless the approved procedure defines a different aggregation rule using more than two evaluators, every required individual evaluation MUST satisfy its predeclared acceptance criteria for the obligation to receive `Pass` from Representative Evaluation.

**PROCESS-EVALUATION-PANEL-004** — An alternative aggregation rule MUST be defined and approved before observations begin and MUST NOT reduce the panel below two qualified evaluators.

**PROCESS-AMBIGUITY-001** — Every statement containing `MUST` or `MUST NOT` MUST be restated independently by two reviewers, including at least one reviewer who has no access to the originating interview context.

**PROCESS-AMBIGUITY-002** — Each independent restatement MUST identify the obligation, at least one compliant outcome, at least one non-compliant outcome, a feasible verification approach, and material dependencies.

**PROCESS-AMBIGUITY-003** — Materially different interpretations MUST block acceptance of the affected identifier until the canonical statement or its definitions resolve the difference.

**PROCESS-AMBIGUITY-COVERAGE-001** — Every stable identifier, including goals, preferences, permissions, non-goals, and deferred capabilities, MUST receive one ambiguity-checklist review.

**PROCESS-AMBIGUITY-COVERAGE-002** — A stable identifier not containing `MUST` or `MUST NOT` MUST receive a second independent review when its first review identifies a material ambiguity, conflict, unverifiable boundary, or risk of changing architecture or accepted scope.

### Baseline acceptance

**PROCESS-BASELINE-MANDATORY-001** — A baseline MUST NOT be approved unless every included `MUST` and `MUST NOT` obligation has an accepted `Pass` disposition and none has a `Blocked` disposition.

**PROCESS-BASELINE-PREFERENCE-001** — A `SHOULD` or `SHOULD NOT` obligation that does not have an accepted `Pass` disposition MUST have an explicitly justified project-owner-approved exception before baseline approval.

**PROCESS-BASELINE-PERMISSION-001** — A `MAY` obligation MUST receive implementation verification only when the permitted capability is included in the candidate baseline; omission of the capability MUST NOT constitute failure.

**PROCESS-BASELINE-SCOPE-001** — Non-goals and deferred capabilities MUST be inspected to confirm that baseline approval does not treat them as required or claim them as accepted capabilities.

### Assignment Expansion Inventory

The generated [Assignment Expansion Inventory](training-simulation-verification-assignment-inventory.csv) is the canonical expanded record for this plan version. Its content-derived inventory version binds the exact initial-requirements and verification-plan contents used for expansion. `AR-nnn` identifies the corresponding assignment-table row in canonical order within that bound plan version.

The inventory expands every range, retains every matching row, records the accumulated Required and Supporting methods, references each applicable required-evidence statement, and records evidence owners and final approvers. Its default-method field records each default as `Accumulated`, `Accumulated as Supporting`, or `Not Applicable`; there are no current Verification Method Exceptions.

The same inventory is the identifier-level Representative Evaluation applicability baseline. `Required Within Identifier` means at least one obligation in the identifier requires the method; it does not apply that method to independently objective obligations in a compound identifier. `Not Required for Identifier` means objective criteria and reproducible technical evidence are sufficient for every obligation. Ambiguity findings are classified `Not Applicable — Metadata`. Each verification procedure replaces this coarse classification with stable obligation-key decisions before approval, preserving mixed decisions separately.
